Maqam Tuning Review

These eight maqamat are drafts in quarter tones (24-tone equal temperament). Please listen to each note and say whether it is in tune for the maqam. Tap a note to hear it. Turn on the drone to hear every note against the tonic.

Cents are measured from the tonic: 100 cents is a semitone, 50 is a quarter tone. Each maqam is played on its traditional tonic.
